Your Custom Gown Design Process

From your first idea to a gown created especially for you

Your gown is designed around your vision, your figure, your personality, your event, and the way you want to feel.

1. Private Discovery Call

We begin with a friendly conversation about your event, date, location, style, and vision. You can share your ideas, inspiration, preferred fabrics, and the feeling you want your gown to create. Together, we discuss the design, fit, comfort, support, timeline, and investment. During this call, I usually create an initial sketch based on your description and desired details. We then discuss the next steps and arrange your in-person appointment.

2. In-Person Design and Measurement Appointment

At our in-person appointment, we review and finalize your sketch together. We confirm the silhouette, fabric, colour, details, construction, comfort, and support. If fabric samples are available, we review them together and make any final design changes needed. We take approximately 50 measurements to create a pattern designed specifically for your figure. When needed, additional measurements may be taken for custom-built cups and corsetry. We also review the timeline and contract. The 50% deposit is usually paid at this appointment.

3. Your Gown Is Created

Once your design and measurements are confirmed, I begin creating your gown. I craft your pattern, prepare or order the fabric, and carefully construct your gown by hand. Every detail is created specifically for you. Your gown is not copied or mass-produced. During the creative process, I can share photographs or videos of your gown if you would like updates. If an important design decision needs to be confirmed, I will contact you before continuing.

4. Fitting and Final Refinements

When your gown is ready, you try it on in person so we can check the fit, comfort, support, length, movement, and overall look. Depending on the design, more than one fitting may be needed. I always aim to keep the process as simple as possible, and many gowns can be completed with one main fitting. If adjustments are needed, I make the refinements and schedule another fitting when appropriate. Once your gown is finished and you are happy with the result, we arrange delivery. The remaining 50% balance, plus applicable taxes, is due upon delivery of the finished gown.

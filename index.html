Step-By-Step

Your Custom Gown Design Process

From your first idea to a gown created especially for you

PHASE 01

Private Discovery Call

We begin with a conversation about your event, date, location, style, and vision. During this call, I create an initial sketch based on your ideas and we outline the timeline, investment, and next steps.

PHASE 02

Design & Measurements

In person, we finalize your sketch, silhouette, fabrics, and colours. We take approximately 50 measurements—including custom cup and corsetry specs—and confirm your contract with a 50% deposit.

PHASE 03

Your Gown Is Created

I draft your personal pattern, prepare the textiles, and construct your gown by hand. Every detail is created from scratch—never copied or mass-produced—with progress photos shared along the way.

PHASE 04

Fitting & Refinements

You try on your gown in person to verify fit, comfort, movement, and hem length. Once any final refinements are completed and you love every detail, the remaining 50% balance is due upon delivery.
